Claim
-----
The Februus defense does not significantly reduce the attack success rate (ASR) of the Flashy backdoor, although clean accuracy remains high.

How this maps to the paper
--------------------------
Section 6.3.3 and Table 9 illustrate that, while Februus removes certain types of triggers, the Flashy backdoor is resilient, and ASR remains high despite the defense.

What this script does
--------------------
- Runs the SNN model with the Flashy backdoor applied.
- Applies the Februus defense during inference.
- Records metrics including:
    - 'clean_acc': accuracy on clean test samples
    - 'asr': attack success rate on poisoned samples
    - 'rounded versions': accuracy and ASR after rounding inputs to nearest integer values instead of leaving them as floats
- Saves the metrics to 'outputs/februus_result.csv' for comparison with the expected results in 'expected/expected_februus_result.csv'.
