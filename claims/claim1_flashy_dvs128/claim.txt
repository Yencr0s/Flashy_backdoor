Claim
-----
Flashy Backdoor achieves near perfect ASR with minimal drop in clean accuracy on DVS128-Gesture using full frame, short strobing triggers at the start or middle of the sequence.

How this maps to the paper
--------------------------
Tables 2 and 4 show 100 percent ASR with 3 flashes and full frame size with small clean accuracy drop. See also Section 6.1.3 for summary of Flashy results.

What this script does
----------------------
- Runs a scaled-down training and eval job of both strobing and static versions.
- Writes claim1.csv with the metrics in outputs/.
