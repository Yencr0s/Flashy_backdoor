Claim
-----
While pruning does not significantly reduce the ASR, it enables Fine-pruning to focus on more influential neurons, leading to a larger drop in ASR compared to fine tuning alone.
And the additional tuning epochs in fine-pruning helps to recover clean accuracy.

How this maps to the paper
---------------------------
Section 6.3.1 and Table 7 show that pruning followed by fine tuning yields stronger reductions in ASR than fine tuning alone in many cases.

What this script does
---------------------
- Runs three configs representing pruning, FT and FP and writes their metrics to outputs/.
- Produces a summary csv for easy comparison.
